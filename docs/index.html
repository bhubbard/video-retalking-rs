video-retalking-rs — 3-Stage Semantic Expression Neutralization & Lip-Sync in Rust

video-retalking-rs is a pure Rust port of VideoReTalking that edits talking-head videos in the wild to match new speech audio regardless of the speaker's original expression. It decouples the pipeline into canonical expression neutralization, audio-driven lip synthesis, and identity-preserving face restoration.
